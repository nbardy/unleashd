## What changed
- `server/src/ingest/boot.ts` `bootIngest`: runs `Ingest.start(defaultRoots(home), <appData>/ingest.sqlite)`, logs the scan, calls `provideIngest`, builds the list, then forwards `onChange` to it.
- `server/src/ingest/conversation-list.ts`:
  - Pure join `listingFor(record, sessions)` returns a sum: `listed` | `no_transcript` | `no_cwd`.
  - Row fields: label is the current session's title, else the first session's label. createdAt is the transcript birth for `external_discovered` records. activityAt is the max over sessions. messageCount is the sum.
  - `onChange` pages `listSessions({since: rev})` and broadcasts `label`/`activity` patches. It sends `rows` for a newly listed conversation and `removed` for one that stops being listed.
- `server.ts`: `loadConversations` runs the old loader and the ingest list in parallel. `hello` sends the registry rows plus `listedRows()`. `ready.conversationIds` is the union of both, because the client prunes anything missing from it. `flushState` stops ingest.
- Runtimes the loader holds keep their own row, run state and bodies (`isLive`). The list stays silent about their ids.
- Rows with no cwd in the record or in any transcript (the Muse approval-review children) are left out. One warn line reports the count.
- Fix-guards: a reason header and `Pattern: one-write-path` / `patches-not-snapshots` tags, plus a patterns.md entry.
- Test `server/test/ingest-list.test.ts`: the real crate over a fixture HOME and a real records store. It checks the rows, the Muse no-cwd exclusion, that live ids go to `ready` only, and that an append arrives as an `activity` patch.
