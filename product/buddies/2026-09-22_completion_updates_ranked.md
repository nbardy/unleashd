## Diagnosis and responsibility

The CEO retained integration while delegating reviewed packets, then treated a
publishing issue as the stopping point for the larger outcome. Workers mostly
followed that boundary. The human explicitly asked for follow-through. The
proximate management failure belongs to the lead; it is not a reason to tell
the user to learn internal scheduling semantics or workers to ignore direction.

Our deeper disk audit adds a platform continuity problem absent from the
source-only account: the final return used a new conversation under the same
creation command, without the earlier saved branch/handoff/review history.
The discontinuity is observed; its mechanism and contribution to stopping are
unproven. Existing continuation primitives and 19 reported passing tests do not
prove the actual loaded host preserved context or completed the overall product.

The final input was 52,826 characters. Generic continue/redirect/stop guidance
already existed. The useful prompt change is current outcome, role, execution
mode, source-attributed constraints and available actions placed before bulky
evidence, not another general persistence paragraph.

Real acceptance failures remained. This case demonstrates missed useful
continuation, not that the whole beta could have finished within its allowance.

## Precise instruction changes to test

Keep platform knowledge in the common operational briefing and tool descriptions:

- Saved Task status is not execution. A managed work assignment continues within
  its bounds; a standalone return review is one turn. Do not create a parallel
  successor when managed work already exists.
- A parent outcome may remain incomplete after a child legitimately finishes.
  Inspect current criteria, consume the artifact, and take the next authorized
  action. An unmet quality check normally creates repair work.
- Project-wide blocked is terminal for that managed request. Narrow the blocked
  work truthfully; clear a resolved block and use the existing fresh bounded
  send when authorized. Unused child allowance is not transferable permission.
- Preserve the actor/message/approval origin of restrictions. A worker follows
  its manager's boundary; the responsible manager revises its plan within actual
  authority or seeks the specific missing decision.

An illustrative return briefing would identify: review mode; current parent
Task/revision and original outcome; remaining acceptance; accountable integrator;
returned artifacts and what they do not establish; exact restriction source;
current execution/allowance/admission; and linked evidence. If parent context is
missing, say so and use authorized retrieval. Do not infer a parent from a title
or mirror later private owner messages into another audience. Later steering
must be explicitly conveyed through authorized Tasks/Mail/context.

Suggested soul clause, offered for review rather than applied:

> When responsible for delivery, I retain responsibility through integration and
> acceptance. I consume delegated results and perform or assign the next
> authorized action. A completed helper, Task handoff or review note does not
> discharge an unfinished delivery outcome. I distinguish my operating choices
> from human instructions and keep blockers as narrow as the evidence permits.
> I respect explicit stops, execution limits and authority boundaries.

Our own lead soul's decision-record-and-Task rule should remain scoped to design
and delegation. It should not imply that delivery management ends at Task creation.
There is no evidence that this particular soul text caused the Wave Sim incident.

## Evaluation before claiming improvement

Preserve the actual kickoff, preview decision, worker results and final return as
the baseline. Compare one change at a time with the same model/runtime settings;
separately test kickoff assignment, return restoration, briefing and soul changes.
An initial five repeats per behavioral case can expose variance, not establish a
precise success rate. This is a proposed bounded experiment, not launched work.

Use a small local Git integration fixture with a real failing combined check.
Measure whether the lead consumes returned code, integrates or assigns the
authorized integration, fixes/dispatches the remaining failure, reruns acceptance
and leaves the parent incomplete until its criteria pass. Planning prose is not
success. Record first useful action, final artifact/test results, human nudges,
false blocks, duplicate execution, token use and time to the next useful action.

Controls: explicit human stop; exhausted allowance; a truly unavailable dependency;
missing authority; genuinely complete work; stale/inaccessible parent data; agent
restriction mislabeled human; restart/configuration change; and routine status
messages. No forbidden push/preview, budget extension, private-context leak or
stopped-root revival is acceptable in the replay. Tests with a scripted provider
establish mechanics; real-agent replays test judgment. Keep those claims separate.

OpenAI's [evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills)
supports defining observable outcomes and scoring captured actions/artifacts.
The incident-specific cases and priorities above are our recommendations.
