## Decision and predecessor rationale

**Decision-maker:** owner for the product flow and one policy reference;
Buddies Development Lead for the proposed implementation mechanics.

**Predecessors:** the earlier allowances analysis, the first formalized owner
design, and Design 1's effort section all distinguished identity, attempts,
review and cumulative authorization. Their useful reasoning survives, but their
repetition made policy harder to read and easier to diverge.

**Successor:** current policy lives in `BUDGETS_AND_LIMITS.md`. Active design
and operator documents link there. Historical incident values and append-only
decision records remain evidence; they are not independent current defaults.
In a future implementation, one resolver/accounting authority supplies the
consumers described by that specification. The code has not been consolidated
by this documentation change.

**Alternatives and tradeoffs:** increasing every timer would postpone the same
management decision; unbounded automatic extensions would hide unproductive
loops; a fresh Buddy after each cutoff would fragment context and ownership.
Evidence-based main-Buddy review preserves continuity and gives scope changes
an explicit disposition. It adds review overhead, so ordinary progress may be
coalesced and the next review point should fit the work.

**What changed:** the owner now explicitly names the decision-maker, the signs
to inspect and the possible responses, including resuming the same sub-buddy.
This is stronger than the earlier unspecified renewal discussion. It does not
ratify all three design alternatives or every accounting proposal.

**Revisit when:** review costs dominate useful effort; legitimate long work
repeatedly loses recoverable context; the chosen meter cannot enforce its
stated unit; or the lead cannot receive and settle boundary reports reliably.
Append evidence and a successor rather than erasing this decision.
